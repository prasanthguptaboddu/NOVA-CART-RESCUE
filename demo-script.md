# Competition Demo Script: NOVA CART Reliance Engine
**Duration**: 3–5 Minutes  
**Audience**: Executive Jury, Product Leads, Venture Evaluators  

---

## 1. Opening & Problem Diagnosis (0:00 – 1:00)

- **Hook**:
  > *"Judges, NOVA CART does not have an acquisition problem—it has a silent fulfillment hemorrhage. We acquire 120,000 new customers every month, yet only 19.4% ever return for a second order. Why?"*

- **Show Screen**: **Problem Diagnosis Tab**
  - Point to **The Churn Chasm**:
    > *"Look at this empirical case data. When a customer has a smooth first order, 42.1% convert to Order #2. But when they experience a phantom stockout or delivery delay, repeat conversion crashes to 8.6%—an 80% destruction of customer retention. 28.6% of our new users experience this friction on Day 1. That is 34,320 customers and ₹1.6 Crores in GMV lost every month."*
  - State the constraint:
    > *"We have a strict ₹25 Lakh budget over 6 months. We cannot build warehouses or buy fleets. We must fix the operational promise digitally. Enter the NOVA CART Reliance Engine."*

---

## 2. Live Dark-Store Dispatch & Friction Triage (1:00 – 2:15)

- **Show Screen**: **Live Operations & Triage Tab**
  - Point to the active dark-store status strip (Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR, Whitefield).
  - Select order **NC-8092** (Customer: Aravind Swaminathan, 1st-Time Buyer):
    > *"Here is Order NC-8092 in Koramangala. The picker Sunil found that the customer's A2 Organic Milk and Brown Eggs are out of stock on the shelf, despite showing available in the app. This is a classic phantom stockout."*
  - **Action**: Click **Auto-Substitute** on the item.
    > *"Watch: with one click, our engine executes an in-stock substitution query, replaces the missing milk with a verified in-stock alternative at zero customer price delta, and updates the database."*
  - **Action**: Click **Preemptively Rescue (₹50 Credit)**.
    > *"Notice the order is approaching an SLA breach. Instead of waiting for Aravind to get angry and file a ₹65 support ticket, our engine executes a preemptive rescue: it deposits ₹50 directly into his Reliance Pass wallet and marks friction neutralized. Look at his churn risk: it instantly drops from 89% to 18%."*

---

## 3. Pre-Checkout Inventory Risk Shield (2:15 – 3:00)

- **Show Screen**: **Inventory Risk Shield Tab**
  - Point to the catalog table:
    > *"How do we prevent this from happening in the first place? Here is our Pre-Checkout Inventory Risk Shield. Every SKU is scored with a phantom risk score based on shrinkage velocity and shelf-to-app drift."*
  - Select the high-risk Epigamia Greek Yogurt (88% phantom risk):
  - **Action**: Click **Audit Shelf**. Change physical count to 15, click **Save & Sync Catalog**.
    > *"The picker conducts a 30-second cycle audit, and the engine immediately synchronizes physical and app inventory, dropping the phantom risk score to 2%."*
  - **Action**: Point to **Quarantine** button.
    > *"If physical stock is depleted, one click quarantines the SKU from consumer carts before a single new customer can order it."*

---

## 4. Second-Order Restock Loop (3:00 – 3:45)

- **Show Screen**: **2nd-Order Restock Loop Tab**
  - Select customer **Aravind Swaminathan**:
    > *"Now, how do we capture Order #2? Look at Aravind's profile. Instead of spraying blanket ₹150 vouchers, our engine tracks his verified delivered items and pantry cycle. Milk has a 3-day cycle, bread has a 4-day cycle. Today is Day 3."*
  - Point to the bill breakdown:
    > *"The engine generates a 1-tap replenishment basket, automatically applying his ₹50 Reliance Pass credit from earlier. Net payable is only ₹290."*
  - **Action**: Click **Simulate Customer One-Tap Restock (Order #2)**.
    > *"Order #2 is placed in SQLite. Aravind is now a retained repeat customer, and his churn score drops to 5%."*

## 5. Financial ROI & Closing (3:45 – 4:30)

- **Show Screen**: **Financial ROI & Impact Tab**
  - Point to the 4 impact cards:

    > *"The Reliance Engine delivers ₹1.43 Crores in 6-month net profit contribution at a 7.7x ROI, paying for itself in just 24 days."*

    > *"NOVA CART doesn't need to burn crores acquiring customers who leave. The Reliance Engine makes every promise reliable."*
